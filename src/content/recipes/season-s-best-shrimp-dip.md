title: SEASON'S BEST SHRIMP DIP
summary: A creamy dip made with Philadelphia cream cheese and Miracle Whip salad dressing, flavored with tiny cocktail shrimp, green onion, and garlic salt.
ingredients:
  - 1 8oz. PKG. PHILADELPHIA BRAND CREAM CHEESE, SOFTENED
  - 1/2 CUP MIRACLE WHIP SALAD DRESSING
  - 1 4¼ oz. CAN TINY COCKTAIL SHRIMP, DRAINED RINSED
  - 1/3 CUP FINELY CHOPPED GREEN ONION
  - 1/8 TSP. GARLIC SALT
instructions:
  - MIX CREAM CHEESE AND SALAD DRESSING UNTIL WELL BLENDED.
  - STIR IN REMAINING INGREDIENTS. CHILL.
  - SERVE WITH VEGETABLE DIPPERS.
notes:
  - "PREP TIME: 10 MINS. PLUS CHILLING."
  - Yields 2 cups.
