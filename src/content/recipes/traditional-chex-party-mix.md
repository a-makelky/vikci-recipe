ingredients:
  - margarine
  - seasoned salt
  - Worcestershire sauce
  - cereals
  - nuts
  - pretzels
instructions:
  - Preheat oven to 250°. In open roasting pan melt margarine in oven. Stir in seasoned salt and Worcestershire sauce. Gradually add cereals, nuts and pretzels, stirring until all pieces are evenly coated. Bake 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes. Spread on absorbent paper to cool. Store in airtight container.
  - In large microwave-safe bowl melt margarine on HIGH 1 minute. Stir in seasoned salt and Worcestershire sauce. Gradually add cereals, nuts and pretzels, stirring until all pieces are evenly coated. Microwave on HIGH 5 to 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes. Spread on absorbent paper to cool. Store in airtight container.
notes:
  - "*Due to differences in microwave ovens, cooking time may need adjustment. These directions were developed using 625 to 700 watt ovens."
  - Makes 9 cups.
