title: Crab Rangoon (Baked)
summary: Baked crab rangoon appetizers made with imitation crab, cream cheese, and mayo.
ingredients:
  - 8oz imitation crab or 1 can crabmeat or imitationcrd
  - 1/4 - 1/2 c. Mayo
  - 1 pkg cream cheese
  - 1/4 - 1/2 c. sliced gr. onion
  - 1 pkg square wonton wrappers (Pasta wrap - Produce section)
instructions:
  - Cream together crab, mayo, cr. cheese + gr. onion.
  - Carefully put wonton wrappers in muffin tin to form 'cup'.
  - Place approx 1 tsp crabmeat mixture in each 'cup'.
  - Bake in 350° oven for 15-18 min. or until wrapper
