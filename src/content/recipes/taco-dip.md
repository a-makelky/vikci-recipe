title: Taco Dip
summary: A layered taco dip made with bean dip, sour cream, cream cheese, vegetables, and cheese, served with Doritos.
ingredients:
  - 1 - 10½ oz cans of Bean Dip
  - 1 pkg taco seasoning mix
  - 1 - 12oz carton Sour cream
  - 1 - 8oz Phil cream cheese
  - Shredded lettuce
  - diced tomatoes
  - shredded American cheese
instructions:
  - Spread 1 - 10½ oz cans of Bean Dip in bottom of tupperware pie taken (pizza pan size).
  - Mix w electric beater - 1 pkg taco seasoning mix, 1 - 12oz carton Sour cream and 1 - 8oz Phil cream cheese.
  - Spread over bean dip.
  - "Layer on: Shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes."
  - Then sprinkle w shredded American cheese.
  - let mellow in fridge for 1 hr.
  - Serve w Doritos.
