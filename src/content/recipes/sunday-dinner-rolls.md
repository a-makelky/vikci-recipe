title: Sunday Dinner Rolls
summary: Soft dinner rolls written on a worn index card with a reminder to brush the tops with butter.
ingredients:
  - 2 cups warm milk
  - 1/2 cup sugar
  - 2 packets yeast
  - 2 eggs
  - 1/2 cup melted butter
  - 5 to 6 cups flour
  - 1 teaspoon salt
instructions:
  - Dissolve the yeast in warm milk with a spoonful of sugar.
  - Stir in eggs, melted butter, remaining sugar, salt, and enough flour to make a soft dough.
  - Let rise, shape into rolls, rise again, and bake at 375F until golden.
notes:
  - The card suggests brushing the tops with butter as soon as the rolls come out.
