title: Breakfast Sausage Rolls
summary: Sausage rolls stuffed with mozzarella cheese, eggs, green pepper, green onion, and Rhodes bread dough.
ingredients:
  - 1 pkg sausage (Jimmy Dean 10 oz.)
  - 1 egg
  - 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  - chopped green pepper
  - chopped green onion
  - 1 loaf Rhodes Bread
instructions:
  - "Do ahead: fry sausage and drain, then scramble egg, add cheese, onion and pepper. Mix all together and put in sealed container for A.M."
  - Roll out bread dough 18 x 8 inch. Spread mix on dough, pinch together/ seam side down. Bake 350° for 35 minutes.
notes:
  - I usually use 4-6 eggs and lots of cheese. I mix cheese with cheddar, feta, mozzarella or whatever.
