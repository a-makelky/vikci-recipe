  - "Mix:"
  - 1 lg. can beans dip
  - 1-8oz sour cream
  - 1/2 pkg taco seasoning
  - 10 drops Tabasco
  - 1-8oz pkg cream cheese
  - Diced onion to taste
instructions:

  - Bake @ 300° until bubbly.
  - Serve with taco or potato chips.
