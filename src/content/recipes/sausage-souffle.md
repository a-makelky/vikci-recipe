title: Sausage Souffle
summary: A savory casserole made with sausage, bread, cheddar cheese, milk, and mushroom soup.
ingredients:
  - 1 lb link sausage
  - 8 slices bread cubed
  - 3/4 lb cheddar cheese, shredded
  - 2 c. milk
  - 1 can mushroom soup
instructions:
  - Fry sausage and drain
  - Cube bread slices
  - Arrange sausage on bread
  - Combine beaten mixture with milk and soup
  - Pour on mixture and let sit overnight
  - Bake @ 350° for 1½ hrs
  - Or until knife moistened comes out clean
