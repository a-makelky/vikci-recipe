title: Fudgy Brownies
summary: A chocolate brownie recipe with the handwritten note to stop baking as soon as the middle sets.
ingredients:
  - 1/2 cup butter
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 2 eggs
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla
  - 1/3 cup cocoa
  - 1/2 cup flour
  - 1/4 teaspoon salt
instructions:
  - Melt the butter and stir in the sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  - Add cocoa, flour, and salt and mix until smooth.
  - Spread in a greased pan and bake at 350F until the center barely sets.
notes:
  - The original card says these are best the next day.
