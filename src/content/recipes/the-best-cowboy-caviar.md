title: THE BEST COWBOY CAVIAR
summary: A spicy and savory dip made with vegetables, corn, beans, and served with crackers or tortilla chips.
ingredients:
  - 1/2 cup diced orange bell pepper
  - 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper
  - 1-2 cans of sweet corn;drained
  - 1-2 cans of black beans; drained
  - 1-2 cans of black-eyed peas;drained
  - 1 cup Cilantro; diced fine
  - 1/2 cup diced purple onion
  - 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  - 1/4 tsp Garlic Powder
  - 1 tsp Chili powder
  - 1/2 tsp Salt
  - 1/4 cup sugar
  - 1/2 cup olive oil
  - 1/3 cup white wine vinegar
  - 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (if wanting spice)
instructions:
  - Add all ingredients into a large bowl
  - mix well and place in fridge for a bit before serving.
  - Serve with crackers or scoop tortilla chips.
